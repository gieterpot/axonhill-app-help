# Sign in and sync

<p class="guide-meta">Draft. The Field sign-in screen is verified. Final Office and sign-out checks are still required.</p>

## Sign in

1. Open the correct AxonHill app.
2. On **Field Login** or **Office Login**, enter your work email.
3. Enter your app password.
4. Select **Continue** or **Sign in**.
5. Wait for the home screen to load.

Do not use another employee's account. Your role controls what the app shows.

## Sync the app

1. Make sure the phone or computer has an internet connection.
2. Select the AppSheet sync icon.
3. Wait until the moving sync sign stops.
4. Check that the screen shows the latest information.

Do not close the app while it is syncing.

## If it does not finish

Go to [App will not open or sync](../fix-a-problem/app-will-not-open-or-sync.md).
