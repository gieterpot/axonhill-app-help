# Choose the correct app

<p class="guide-meta">Draft. Last checked against the apps on 19 August 2026.</p>

AxonHill uses two apps.

## Use Axonhill Field when you are working in the field

Use it to:

- start the day;
- record attendance;
- submit harvest counts;
- check your recent counts.

The Field app is made for a phone.

## Use Axonhill Office when you are doing office work

Use it to:

- manage employees, teams and Harvest Groups;
- review counts and worklogs;
- create attendance and payroll reports;
- manage clients, compartments, work types and pay rates.

The Office app is easiest to use on a computer.

## If you opened the wrong app

1. Close it.
2. Open the other AxonHill app.
3. Sign in with your normal app details.
