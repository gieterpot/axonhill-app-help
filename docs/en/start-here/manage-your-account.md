# Change your password or sign out

<p class="guide-meta">Draft. Field sign-out and the Office password-change configuration are verified. A fresh password-change result still needs a controlled test.</p>

## Sign out of the Field app

![Field My Profile screen for the labelled test Counter account](../assets/screenshots/field/sign-out-field-profile.png)

1. Open the Field app menu.
2. Open **My Profile**.
3. Select **Logout**.
4. Check that **Field Login** opens.

Sign out when another person will use the same device.

## Sign out of the Office app

1. Open **My Profile**.
2. Select **Log Out**.
3. Check that **Office Login** opens.

## Change your Office password

1. Open **My Profile** in the Office app.
2. Select **Change Password**.
3. Enter your **Current Password**.
4. Enter a **New Password** with at least eight characters.
5. Enter the same password under **Confirm New Password**.
6. Select **Save** once.
7. Wait for the result to show **Success**.

Use the new password the next time you sign in.

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Password must be at least 8 characters.** | Enter a longer new password. |
| **Passwords do not match.** | Enter the same new password in both new-password fields. |
| The result stays **Pending** | Sync the app and wait for the result before trying again. |
| You do not know the current password | Ask an Admin or Office user to reset it. |
