# Confirm incoming stock

<p class="guide-meta">Checked with a controlled test transfer on 20 August 2026.</p>

Use **Incoming stock** when stock has physically arrived at your location.

1. Open **Stock**, then **Incoming stock**.
2. Find the correct transfer. Check the source, destination, item and quantity.
3. Open the transfer.
4. Select **Confirm receipt of stock**.
5. The confirmation may say only **Are you sure?**. Recheck the open transfer before continuing.
6. Confirm once, then allow the app to sync.
7. Return to **Incoming stock** and check that the transfer no longer requires receipt.

Do not confirm stock that has not physically arrived. If the item, quantity or destination is wrong, stop and send the transfer details to the Office.
