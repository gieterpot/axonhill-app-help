# View stock and equipment

<p class="guide-meta">Checked with the H&amp;S Field menu on 20 August 2026.</p>

Use **Stock** to check stock, equipment and work that is due.

## Open the correct list

1. Sign in with an H&S account.
2. Open **Stock**.
3. Choose the list you need:

   - **My Stock** shows quantities at your locations;
   - **Stock to reorder** shows items at or below their reorder level;
   - **My Equipment** shows tracked equipment at your locations;
   - **Inspections and services due** shows equipment needing attention;
   - **My Locations** shows the stores or vehicles for which you are responsible.

4. Select a row to see more detail.
5. Sync before relying on a quantity or due date.

The current lists are wide on a phone. Swipe sideways when a column is cut off. Check the location carefully if you control more than one store or vehicle.

## If something looks wrong

Do not correct a quantity by issuing test stock. Record the item, size or model, location and visible quantity, then send those details to the Office.
