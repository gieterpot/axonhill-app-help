# Understand harvest count statuses

<p class="guide-meta">Draft. The status meanings are verified from the current workflow. A fresh controlled transition still needs screenshots.</p>

Open **My Recent Entries** for normal counts or **My Recent Batches** for batch counts. The status tells you what the app is doing.

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | The count is saved but is still waiting for processing. | Keep the app online and sync. Do not submit the same count again. |
| **Processing** | The automation is working on the count. | Wait and sync again. Do not submit a duplicate. |
| **Completed** | Processing finished successfully. | Open the count and check the final details. |
| **Rejected** | The count did not pass a required check. | Open it, read the message and follow the correction instruction. |
| **Error** | Processing failed. | Open it, keep the error message and report it to the office or administrator. |

## If Draft or Processing does not change

1. Check that the device has an internet connection.
2. Select the AppSheet sync icon.
3. Keep the app open until sync finishes.
4. Open **My Recent Entries** or **My Recent Batches** again.
5. If the status is still unchanged after the normal processing period, send the office a screenshot of the count, its status and the time it was submitted.

Do not create another count unless the office tells you to. A second submission can create duplicate worklogs.

## If the status is Completed but the details are wrong

Do not edit or replace the original without instruction. Report the wrong Team, Compartment, Harvest Group, employee, work type or quantity to the office.
