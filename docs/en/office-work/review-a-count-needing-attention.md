# Review a count needing attention

<p class="guide-meta">Draft. The queue and detail are verified. The Override Verification result still needs a controlled test.</p>

Use **Counts Needing Review** for counts that did not complete normal employee verification.

## Check the count

1. Open the Office app.
2. Select **Counts Needing Review**.
3. Find the count by date, employee, Team or Harvest Group.
4. Open the count.
5. Check the compartment, Team and Harvest Group.
6. Check the chainsaw operator, debarker, Marker 1 and Marker 2 where shown.
7. Check every work type, quantity and unit.
8. Read the notes and current status.

## Decide what to do

- If any detail is wrong or unclear, stop and ask the counter or supervisor to confirm it.
- Use **Override Verification** only when you are authorised and have independently confirmed that all displayed details are correct.
- Do not submit another count merely because the first count is still in this queue.

After an approved action, sync and confirm that the count leaves the review queue and its worklogs appear correctly.
