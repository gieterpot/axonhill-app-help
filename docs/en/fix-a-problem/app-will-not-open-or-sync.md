# App will not open or sync

<p class="guide-meta">Draft troubleshooting guide. Last checked on 19 August 2026.</p>

Try these steps in order. Stop when the app works.

## 1. Check the connection

1. Open another website on the same phone or computer.
2. If it does not open, fix the Wi-Fi or mobile-data connection first.

## 2. Wait for the current sync

If the sync sign is still moving, wait one minute. Do not keep pressing sync.

## 3. Close and reopen the app

1. Close the AxonHill app or browser tab.
2. Open it again.
3. Sign in if asked.
4. Let the first screen finish loading.

## 4. Try once more

Select sync once and wait.

## 5. Report the exact error

If the app still fails:

1. Take a screenshot of the whole error.
2. Write the time and which app you used.
3. Say what you were trying to do.
4. Send it to your supervisor or the correct work WhatsApp group.

If the message says **Unable to fetch app definition** or **Timed out waiting for a resource**, include those exact words. This helps the administrator check slow tables or formula cycles.

Do not repeatedly save the same count while the app is stuck. First check **My Recent Entries** or **My Recent Batches** after sync returns.
