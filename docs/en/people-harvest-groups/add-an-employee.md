# Add an employee to a Harvest Group

<p class="guide-meta">Draft. The request form is verified. The final processed result still needs a controlled test.</p>

Use this to give an employee an additional Harvest Group assignment.

## Steps

1. Open the Office app.
2. Open **HR Setup**.
3. Open **Harvesting Groups**.
4. Open the required group.
5. Select **Add Employee**.
6. Check that **Request Type** is **Add Additional Assignment**.
7. Choose the employee.
8. Check the team and Harvest Group.
9. Under **With this role**, choose the employee's role in this group.
10. Choose the effective date.
11. Add a note if needed.
12. Select **Save** once.

The role is required. Do not leave **With this role** blank.

## Check the result

After the app syncs, open the Harvest Group again. The employee should appear as a current member with the selected role.
