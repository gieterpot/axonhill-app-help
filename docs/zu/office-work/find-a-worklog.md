# Thola i-worklog bese ubuka ukubalwa kwayo

<p class="guide-meta">Kusalungiswa. Uhlu, imininingwane nesixhumanisi sokubalwa kuhloliwe. Izithombe zomphakathi zisadinga idatha yokubonisa.</p>

Sebenzisa **All Worklogs** ukuthola umsebenzi womsebenzi oyedwa nokubona ukubalwa owavela kukho.

## Thola i-worklog

1. Vula i-Office app.
2. Khetha **All Worklogs**.
3. Khetha usuku olufanele ohlwini olungakwesobunxele.
4. Thola umugqa womsebenzi.
5. Hlola indima, i-Team, i-Harvest Group, i-compartment, uhlobo lomsebenzi, inani, i-unit ne-status.
6. Vula umugqa ukuze ubone imininingwane.

## Buka ukubalwa okuwumthombo

1. Emininingwaneni ye-worklog, khetha **View Count Verification**.
2. Qhathanisa i-worklog nabasebenzi, izinhlobo zomsebenzi namanani akukubalwa.
3. Buyela ku-worklog uma usuqedile.

Ungasebenzisi **Edit** uma ubuka kuphela. Ifomu lamanje liqukethe izinkambu zangaphakathi futhi kufanele lisetshenziselwe kuphela ukulungisa okulawulwayo.
