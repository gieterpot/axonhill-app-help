# Faka umsebenzi ku-Harvest Group

<p class="guide-meta">Kusalungiswa. Ifomu le-request lihloliwe. Umphumela wokugcina usadinga ukuhlolwa.</p>

Sebenzisa lokhu ukuze unikeze umsebenzi enye i-Harvest Group assignment.

![I-HR Setup enethayela le-Harvesting Groups](../assets/screenshots/office/choose-app-office-hr-setup.png)

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula i-Office app.
2. Vula **HR Setup**.
3. Vula **Harvesting Groups**.
4. Vula i-group efanele.
5. Khetha **Add Employee**.
6. Hlola ukuthi **Request Type** ithi **Add Additional Assignment**.
7. Khetha umsebenzi.
8. Hlola i-team ne-Harvest Group.
9. Ku-**With this role**, khetha umsebenzi azowenza kule group.
10. Khetha usuku aqala ngalo.
11. Faka i-note uma idingeka.
12. Khetha **Save** kanye kuphela.

Umsebenzi wakhe uyadingeka. Ungashiyi **With this role** ingenalutho.

## Hlola umphumela

Uma uhlelo seluvumelanisile, vula i-Harvest Group futhi. Umsebenzi kufanele abonakale njengelungu lamanje nomsebenzi owukhethile.
