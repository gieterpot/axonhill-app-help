# Buka amalungu amanje e-Harvest Group

<p class="guide-meta">Lo mzila uhlolwe ngaphambi nangemuva kokushintsha umsebenzi nge-Harvest Group yokuhlola ehlukile ngomhlaka 19 Agasti 2026. Umbhalo wesiZulu usadinga ukuhlolwa umuntu okhuluma isiZulu kahle.</p>

Sebenzisa lokhu uma ufuna abantu abasebenza kule Harvest Group manje. Ama-assignment amadala awaboniswa lapha.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula i-Office app.
2. Vula **HR Setup**.
3. Vula **Harvesting Groups**.
4. Vula i-Harvest Group efanele.
5. Khetha **Current members**.
6. Hlola umsebenzi negama ngaphansi kwesihloko somsebenzi ngamunye.

![Amalungu amanje e-Harvest Group yokuhlola](../assets/screenshots/office/hg-current-members.png)

## Uma ilungu lingabonakali

Hlola ukuthi i-assignment iyasebenza nokuthi usuku lokuqala selufikile. Uma umuntu kufanele abe kule group manje, sebenzisa i-assignment request efanele. Ungashintshi i-history endala.

Sebenzisa **Full membership history** uma ufuna ukubona ama-assignment amadala noma aphelile.
