# Shintsha osebenzisa i-chainsaw ku-Harvest Group

<p class="guide-meta">Kusalungiswa. Ifomu nohlu lwabasebenzi kuhloliwe. Umphumela wokugcina usadinga ukuhlolwa.</p>

Sebenzisa lokhu uma umuntu oyedwa eyeka ukuba i-chainsaw operator ye-group, omunye athathe indawo yakhe.

Umuntu omdala uyasuswa ku-Harvest Group. Sebenzisa ukushintsha umsebenzi okuvamile uma umuntu omdala kufanele ahlale kule group enza omunye umsebenzi.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula i-Office app.
2. Vula **HR Setup**.
3. Vula **Harvesting Groups**.
4. Vula i-group efanele.
5. Khetha **Add Employee**.
6. Ku-**Request Type**, khetha **Replace Chainsaw Operator**.
7. Ku-**Employee**, khetha i-chainsaw operator entsha.
8. Khetha usuku aqala ngalo.
9. Faka i-note uma idingeka.
10. Khetha **Save** kanye kuphela.

Uhlu lubonisa abasebenzi abasebenzayo abane-default role ethi **Chainsaw Operator**. Lungabandakanya abantu bakwamanye ama-team, ngakho hlola igama kahle.

## Hlola umphumela

Uma uhlelo seluvumelanisile, vula i-Harvest Group futhi. Hlola ukuthi:

- umuntu omusha uyi-**Chainsaw Operator** yamanje;
- umuntu wangaphambili akaselona ilungu lamanje lale Harvest Group.
