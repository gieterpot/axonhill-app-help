# Umsebenzi, i-Team noma i-Harvest Group ayibonakali

<p class="guide-meta">Kusalungiswa. Imithetho yohlu lwamanje ihloliwe. Ukuhlolwa komphumela wokugcina kusasebenzisa ama-test record alawulwayo.</p>

Sebenzisa lokhu uma umuntu, i-Team noma i-Harvest Group ingaveli efomini le-assignment noma le-count.

## Hlola lokhu kuqala

1. Qinisekisa ukuthi idivayisi ine-internet.
2. Yenza i-sync.
3. Vala ifomu bese ulivula futhi.
4. Hlola ukuthi ukhethe i-request type efanele.

## I-Harvest Group ayibonakali

| Okubonayo | Imbangela engenzeka | Okufanele ukwenze |
|---|---|---|
| I-Team ikhethiwe kodwa i-group ayikho | Uhlu lubonisa ama-group asebenzayo ale Team kuphela. | Hlola i-Team. Cela ihhovisi lihlole ukuthi i-Harvest Group iyasebenza futhi ingeyale Team. |
| I-Team ayikhethiwe | Uhlu kufanele lubonise wonke ama-Harvest Group asebenzayo. | Sesha ngegama eligcwele le-group, bese ukhetha inhlanganisela efanele ye-Team ne-operator. |
| I-group ayisebenzi | Ama-group angasebenzi awaveli kuma-assignment amasha. | Cela i-admin iqinisekise ukuthi i-group kufanele isebenze yini. |

## Umsebenzi akabonakali

| I-request type | Ubani okufanele abonakale | Okufanele uhlole |
|---|---|---|
| **Add Additional Assignment** noma **Move Assignment** | Abasebenzi abasebenzayo | Hlola ukuthi umsebenzi umakwe ukuthi active. |
| **Remove Assignment** | Abasebenzi abane-Team membership esebenzayo | Hlola ukuthi umsebenzi usenayo i-assignment ofuna ukuyisusa. |
| **Replace Chainsaw Operator** | Abasebenzi abasebenzayo abane-default role ethi **Chainsaw Operator** | Hlola i-default role futhi uqinisekise ukuthi lowo operator useyilungu elisebenzayo le-Team okuyiwa kuyo. Uhlu lwamanje aluhloli i-Team. |
| **Change Harvest Group Role** | Amalungu akhona e-Harvest Group ekhethiwe | Vula ilungu ku-group efanele ngaphambi kokuqala i-request. |

Ungakhethi **New** ku-dropdown ukuze uzame ukulungisa i-record engekho. Lokho kungadala umsebenzi, i-role noma i-Harvest Group ephindwe kabili noma engaphelele.

Ku-Field attendance, i-Supervisor kufanele futhi ibe ne-Team membership esebenzayo. Ukuyikhetha kuphela enkambini ye-Supervisor ye-Team akuyenzi i-Team ivele ku-**Start Day**.

## I-role ayikhethiwe noma iyanqatshwa

Yonke i-Harvest Group membership efakiwe, ethuthiwe noma eshintshiwe idinga **With this role**. Khetha i-role ngaphambi kokugcina.

Uma ukucutshungulwa kwehluleka, vula i-request ukopishe wonke umlayezo ku-**Message**. Thumela i-request type, umsebenzi, i-Team, i-Harvest Group nomlayezo ku-admin.
