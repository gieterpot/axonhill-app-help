# Qinisekisa i-stock engenayo

<p class="guide-meta">Kuhlolwe nge-transfer yokuhlola ngo-20 Agasti 2026.</p>

Sebenzisa **Incoming stock** uma i-stock isifikile ngempela endaweni yakho.

1. Vula **Stock**, bese uvula **Incoming stock**.
2. Thola i-transfer efanele. Hlola lapho ivela khona, lapho iya khona, into nenani.
3. Vula i-transfer.
4. Khetha **Confirm receipt of stock**.
5. Isiqinisekiso singasho kuphela **Are you sure?**. Phinda uhlole i-transfer ngaphambi kokuqhubeka.
6. Qinisekisa kanye kuphela, bese uvumela uhlelo luqede i-sync.
7. Buyela ku-**Incoming stock** uhlole ukuthi i-transfer ayisadingi ukuqinisekiswa.

Ungaqinisekisi i-stock engakafiki. Uma into, inani noma indawo kungalungile, yima uthumele imininingwane ye-transfer ehhovisi.
