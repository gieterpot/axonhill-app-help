# Buka i-stock nemishini

<p class="guide-meta">Kuhlolwe nge-menu ye-H&amp;S ku-Field app ngo-20 Agasti 2026.</p>

Sebenzisa **Stock** ukuhlola i-stock, imishini nomsebenzi okufanele wenziwe.

## Vula uhlu olufanele

1. Ngena nge-account ye-H&S.
2. Vula **Stock**.
3. Khetha uhlu oludingayo:

   - **My Stock** ibonisa amanani ezindaweni zakho;
   - **Stock to reorder** ibonisa izinto ezidinga uku-odwa;
   - **My Equipment** ibonisa imishini elandelwayo ezindaweni zakho;
   - **Inspections and services due** ibonisa imishini edinga ukunakwa;
   - **My Locations** ibonisa ama-store noma izimoto oziphethe.

4. Khetha umugqa ukuze ubone imininingwane.
5. Yenza i-sync ngaphambi kokuthemba inani noma usuku.

Uhlu lwamanje lubanzi efonini. Swayipha eceleni uma ikholomu isikiwe. Hlola indawo kahle uma uphethe izindawo ezingaphezu kweyodwa.

## Uma kukhona okungalungile

Ungazami ukulungisa inani ngokukhipha i-stock yokuhlola. Bhala into, usayizi noma imodeli, indawo nenani elibonakalayo, bese uthumela leyo mininingwane ehhovisi.
