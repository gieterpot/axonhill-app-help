# Thola bese wabelana ngosizo

<p class="guide-meta">Kusalungiswa. Izinkinobho zakhiwe, kodwa kusadingeka ukuhlolwa kokugcina kufoni ye-Android.</p>

Umhlahlandlela ngamunye uchaza umsebenzi owodwa. Akudingeki ufunde yonke indawo yosizo.

## Thola umhlahlandlela

1. Vula **AxonHill App Help**.
2. Khetha uphawu lokusesha.
3. Bhala igama elifushane, njenge-`count`, `attendance`, `HG`, `employee` noma `sync`.
4. Vula umhlahlandlela ohambisana nomsebenzi wakho.

Ungavula nesigaba kwimenyu.

## Shintsha ulimi

Phezulu komhlahlandlela, khetha **Funda ngesiZulu** ukuze uvule umhlahlandlela ofanayo ngesiZulu.

Ekhasini lesiZulu, khetha **Funda nge-English** ukuze ubuyele ku-English.

## Yabelana ngomhlahlandlela

Phezulu komhlahlandlela, khetha okukodwa:

- **Share** ivula indawo ejwayelekile yefoni yokwabelana, uma i-browser iyisekela.
- **Share on WhatsApp** ivula i-WhatsApp inesihloko nesixhumanisi somhlahlandlela.
- **Copy link** ikopisha isixhumanisi ukuze usifake emlayezweni.

Hlola umlayezo ngaphambi kokuwuthumela.

## Landa umhlahlandlela ukuze uwusebenzise ngaphandle kwe-internet

Imihlahlandlela evunyelwe yokubala ne-attendance ibonisa **Landa ukuze usebenzise ngaphandle kwe-internet** phezulu.

1. Khetha **Landa ukuze usebenzise ngaphandle kwe-internet**.
2. Thumela ifayela le-HTML elilandiwe nge-WhatsApp, noma uligcine efonini.
3. Vula ifayela ku-browser uma kungekho signal.

Ifayela elilandiwe liyikhophi egciniwe. Uma usunayo i-internet futhi, sebenzisa umhlahlandlela oku-internet ukuze ubone iziqondiso zakamuva.
