# Khetha uhlelo olufanele

<p class="guide-meta">Kusalungiswa. Kugcine ukubhekwa ezinhlelweni ngomhlaka 19 Agasti 2026.</p>

I-AxonHill inezinhlelo ezimbili.

## Sebenzisa i-Axonhill Field uma usebenza ehlathini

![I-Field Login ye-AxonHill efonini](../assets/screenshots/field/sign-in-field-login.png)

Yisebenzise ukuze:

- uqale usuku;
- ubhale ukuya emsebenzini;
- uthumele ukubalwa komsebenzi;
- ubheke ukubalwa kwakho kwakamuva.

I-Field app yenzelwe ifoni.

## Sebenzisa i-Axonhill Office uma wenza umsebenzi wasehhovisi

![I-Axonhill Office ibonisa i-HR Setup kukhompyutha](../assets/screenshots/office/choose-app-office-hr-setup.png)

Yisebenzise ukuze:

- ulawule abasebenzi, ama-team nama-Harvest Group;
- uhlole ukubalwa nama-worklog;
- wenze imibiko ye-attendance ne-payroll;
- ulawule ama-client, ama-compartment, ama-work type nama-pay rate.

I-Office app ilula kakhulu kukhompyutha.

## Uma uvule uhlelo olungalungile

1. Luvale.
2. Vula olunye uhlelo lwe-AxonHill.
3. Ngena ngemininingwane yakho evamile.
